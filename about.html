About Lumina Center • Founded 2014

Where Neurodiversity Meets Compassionate Clinical Rigor.

Lumina Center was established with an uncompromising mandate: to replace deficit-based intervention with a neurodiversity-affirming academy where children with Autism, ADHD, sensory integration differences, speech delays, and Down syndrome unlock self-efficacy, academic triumph, and lifelong social belonging.

Collaborative Synergy

The Co-Treatment Advantage: Breaking Therapy Silos

Traditional clinics force parents to shuttle exhausted children between disjointed providers who never speak to one another. At Lumina, our entire clinical faculty works together under one roof, orchestrating synchronized care plans.

1 Occupational + Speech Simultaneous Integration

When a child's vestibular and proprioceptive systems are regulated on a therapeutic swing, their speech motor centers are significantly more receptive to vocal articulation drills.

2 Universal Design for Learning (UDL) in Every Classroom

Lessons offer multi-modal engagement: auditory recordings, visual infographics, and physical building blocks, allowing non-traditional thinkers to demonstrate mastery without academic anxiety.

3 Family Co-Partnering & Daily Transparent Data

Parents receive HIPAA-compliant daily clinical video logs and IEP progression charts, ensuring breakthroughs made in sensory gyms transfer directly to family dinner tables.

Adaptive Montessori & Sensory Manipulatives Wing Universal Design Accredited

Neurodevelopment is a continuous journey. Our cohorts provide scaffolding tailored to distinct physiological and cognitive life stages.
