Unified Care Architecture

Why Siloed Therapy Fails—And How Lumina Co-Treatment Works.

In traditional clinics, children see a speech therapist on Monday, an occupational therapist on Wednesday, and a behavior specialist on Friday—each with disjointed goals. At Lumina, specialists conduct simultaneous co-treatment sessions.

1

Weekly Interdisciplinary Grand Rounds

All therapists on a child's care team meet every Thursday to review video milestone captures, calibrate sensory thresholds, and adjust IEP benchmarks.

2

Gym-Based SLP + OT Co-Treatment

Speech pathology is delivered while the child is engaged in vestibular swing movement and proprioceptive compression, maximizing neurological language retrieval.

3

Unified Daily Family Progress Briefs

Parents receive a single consolidated clinical note with video evidence daily, eliminating contradictory therapist advice and homework fatigue.

Grand Rounds Clinical Protocol HIPAA Compliant

Every therapeutic recommendation is cross-validated by at least two licensed faculty members across speech, sensory processing, and neurodevelopment before submission to school district IEP teams.
