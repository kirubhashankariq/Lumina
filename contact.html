Visiting Preparedness

Low-Arousal Campus Walkthrough Protocols.

Visiting a new environment can trigger anxiety for sensory-sensitive children. We provide proactive accommodations so your child feels secure and welcomed from the moment they step into our building.

01

Advance Visual Schedule Sent to Child

Prior to your arrival, our intake coordinator sends a customized photo storybook showing the entrance, the sensory decompression room, and the smiling faces of the therapists you will meet.

Predictability First
02

Sensory Welcome Kit at Reception

Upon entry, your child is offered noise-canceling headphones, weighted lap plushies, seamless fidgets, and access to our dimmable vestibule without fluorescent flicker.

Immediate Comfort
03

Child-Led Exploration Pace

Tours are unhurried. If your child wants to explore the sensory swing gym for 20 minutes or pause in the quiet sensory garden, our therapists follow their pace and comfort level.

Zero Pressure

Emergency Clinical Guidance

Experiencing an Acute Pediatric Behavioral or Sensory Crisis?

While Lumina provides outpatient and educational day academy services rather than acute psychiatric inpatient hospitalization, our on-call clinical triage coordinator can direct families to accredited regional pediatric neuro-emergency crisis centers 24/7.
