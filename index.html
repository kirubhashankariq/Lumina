The Co-Treatment Advantage

Four Disciplines. One Unified Individual Plan.

Traditional models isolate speech, occupational therapy, behavior, and classroom teaching into separate disjointed visits. At Lumina, our clinical specialists co-treat side-by-side in real time.

OT

Occupational Therapy (OTR/L)

Focuses on neuromuscular regulation, vestibular/proprioceptive processing, fine-motor pencil grip, sensory balance, and self-care independence.

✓ Regulation & Sensory Processing
SLP

Speech Pathology (CCC-SLP)

Develops receptive/expressive language, dynamic AAC system fluency, vocal articulation, pediatric feeding therapy, and pragmatic social communication.

✓ Communication & Voice Empowerment
BCBA

Neuro-Affirming Behavior (BCBA)

Identifies root stressors, antecedent accommodations, and positive reinforcement to replace distress behaviors with autonomy and self-regulation.

✓ Emotional Safety & Self-Advocacy
UDL

Adaptive Special Educators

Translates clinical goals directly into daily academic curricula using Universal Design for Learning (UDL), multimodal instruction, and IEP milestones.

✓ Academic Access & Lifelong Skills

Our Roadmap

The 4-Stage Clinical IEP & Lifelong Growth Journey

From first intake to community independence, our multidisciplinary roadmap ensures continuous measurable progress, transparent family communication, and school transition readiness.

STAGE 01
Days 1 – 14

Multidisciplinary Diagnostic Intake

Comprehensive sensory profiling, speech articulation diagnostics, fine/gross motor evaluation, and cognitive learning assessment conducted by our clinical team.

Deliverable: 360° Neurodevelopmental Baseline Report
STAGE 02
Days 15 – 30

Individual IEP & Goal Setting

Parents, therapists, and educators collaborate to draft an Individualized Education Plan with measurable SMART goals, sensory diets, and classroom accommodations.

Deliverable: Certified IEP & Accommodation Protocol
STAGE 03
Ongoing Daily

1:2 Co-Treatment & Instruction

Daily immersion in small learning pods (1:2 ratio). Speech, OT, and behavior support are integrated seamlessly within experiential academic sessions.

Deliverable: Real-Time Weekly Progress Reports & Video Logs
STAGE 04
Quarterly & Annual

Milestone Review & Transition

Quarterly clinical reviews re-evaluate sensory regulation and academic gains. Transition pathways prepare students for mainstream integration or vocational life.

Deliverable: Transition Readiness & Life-Skills Cert
