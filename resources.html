Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept insurance?

We work seamlessly with a variety of premium PPO providers for specific therapeutic services like highly specialized OT and Speech. Educational tuition is handled entirely separately.

What is the exact student-to-teacher ratio?

We maintain a remarkably strict 1:3 ratio in all core classrooms, with dedicated 1:1 available instantly for intensive, high-focus interventions.
