Diagnostic Observation Architecture

Developmental Screener & Pediatric Milestone Roadmap.

Early identification of neurodivergent communication and sensory processing differences allows children to receive adaptive tools before frustration or school withdrawal develops.

Tier 1: 18m – 3 Years Early Steps

Early Joint Attention & Sensory Markers

  • ▪ Inconsistent response to spoken name without hearing deficits
  • ▪ Gestalt language scripting or echolalia from media/cartoons
  • ▪ Intense distress with common household sounds, textures, or foods
  • ▪ Toe-walking, prolonged spinning, or visual alignment of toys
Recommended: ESDM Play Assessment
Tier 2: 3 – 5 Years Preschool

Communication & Sensory Self-Regulation

  • ▪ Severe difficulty transitioning between activities or environments
  • ▪ Non-speaking, delayed expressive speech, or limited multi-word combinations
  • ▪ Sensory seeking: intense crashing, jumping, or oral fixation
  • ▪ Difficulty holding utensils, scissors, or buttons independently
Recommended: SLP AAC & OT Gym Battery
Tier 3: 6 – 11 Years Primary School

Executive Function & Classroom UDL

  • ▪ Struggles with working memory, task initiation, and multi-step directions
  • ▪ Persistent phonological reversal, letter confusion, or dysgraphia
  • ▪ Sensory overload meltdowns during cafeteria or recess periods
  • ▪ Difficulty understanding peer social cues and collaborative games
Recommended: Full Multi-Domain IEP Review
Tier 4: 12 – 18 Years Secondary / Transition

Self-Advocacy & Life Independence

  • ▪ Executive dysfunction with long-term academic projects and schedules
  • ▪ Social masking fatigue, academic burnout, and generalized anxiety
  • ▪ Need for structured vocational adaptations and public transit navigation
  • ▪ Building independent medical management and legal self-advocacy
Recommended: Transition IEP & ITP Audit

Family Answers & Admissions Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions from Prospective Parents.

Clear, transparent details regarding school district funding, admissions timelines, clinical therapies, and our neurodiversity-affirming mission.

Lumina is state-certified as an approved Non-Public School (NPS License #NPS-4409). Under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA), when a local school district cannot provide Free Appropriate Public Education (FAPE) in a conventional classroom, the district IEP team can place the student at Lumina. In these cases, tuition and specialized transportation are funded entirely by the placing school district at zero cost to the family. We also offer private pay and partial scholarship options for families seeking direct admission.
Traditional therapy clinics see children for 45 minutes once or twice a week in isolated rooms. Lumina operates as a unified academy where therapy is woven into the entire school day. Speech pathologists, occupational therapists, and behavior specialists co-treat during academic instruction, playground socialization, and lunchtime. Furthermore, we are strictly neurodiversity-affirming—we never force eye contact, suppress natural motor stimming, or demand compliance through non-consensual drills.
To begin our intake review, we request any existing medical diagnostic evaluations (e.g., ADOS-2, Vineland-3, WISC-V, developmental pediatric reports) and current or past school IEP or Section 504 plans. If your child has never undergone formal diagnostic testing, our clinical faculty can administer comprehensive multidisciplinary diagnostic batteries on-site at our campus.
Yes, non-speaking, minimally speaking, and unreliably speaking students thrive at Lumina. Our speech department is nationally recognized for high-tech Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) implementation—including Proloquo2Go, TouchChat, LAMP Words for Life, and eye-gaze systems. Every classroom and therapy gym is equipped with core vocabulary visual boards, and all staff are trained in aided language stimulation.
We operate on a zero-restraint, trauma-informed philosophy. Meltdowns are understood as involuntary neurological responses to overwhelming sensory or communicative demands, never as purposeful misbehavior. When a student shows distress, we immediately reduce environmental sensory inputs (dimming circadian lighting, dampening acoustics) and guide them to our sensory decompression nooks equipped with weighted blankets, dark dens, and deep pressure proprioceptive tools.
While academic tuition is managed through district IEP placements or private enrollment, clinical outpatient therapy services (Speech-Language Pathology, Occupational Therapy, Physical Therapy, and Behavioral Health) are provided with itemized clinical superbills containing medical ICD-10 and CPT procedural codes for direct out-of-network reimbursement through your private health insurance plan.
