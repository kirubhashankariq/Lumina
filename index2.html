Gold-Standard Diagnostics

Evidence-Based Developmental Assessment Batteries

Accurate diagnosis removes guesswork. Our multidisciplinary team utilizes standardized, nationally normed clinical assessments to evaluate motor milestones, sensory reactivity, communication fluency, and social interaction.

Autism Diagnostic ADOS®-2 & CARS-2

Autism Diagnostic Observation Schedule

Gold-standard semi-structured observation evaluating reciprocal social interaction, communication nuances, play behaviors, and restricted interests.

Target Ages: 12 months through adulthood
Sensory Profile Sensory Profile™-2

Dunn’s Sensory Processing Battery

Evaluates auditory, visual, touch, movement, and body position thresholds to pinpoint seeking, avoiding, and hyper/hypo-sensitive sensory patterns.

Target Ages: Birth through 14 years
Speech & Language PLS™-5 & CELF®-P3

Preschool Language Comprehensive Scales

Identifies expressive language delays, receptive understanding gaps, vocabulary emergence, and childhood speech sound articulation difficulties.

Target Ages: Birth through 7 years 11 months
Motor Skills PDMS-2 (Peabody)

Developmental Motor Scales Assessment

Measures reflexes, stationary balance, locomotion, object manipulation, grasping coordination, and visual-motor integration skills.

Target Ages: Birth through 5 years
Infant Development Bayley-4 Battery

Bayley Scales of Infant & Toddler Dev

Comprehensive diagnostic assessment identifying developmental delay across 5 key domains: cognitive, language, motor, socio-emotional, and adaptive.

Target Ages: 16 days to 42 months
Adaptive Behavior Vineland™-3

Vineland Adaptive Behavior Scale

Measures daily personal self-sufficiency, functional communication, community socialization, and motor independence in authentic settings.

Target Ages: Birth to 90 years

Our Clinical Philosophy

Why Lumina Uses Naturalistic Developmental Behavioral Interventions

Traditional behavioral models focused on compliance, repetitive drills, and suppressing harmless self-soothing behaviors. Lumina champions evidence-based NDBI and DIR®/Floortime—where motivation, emotional connection, and child-led play drive authentic neural growth.

The Lumina NDBI Standard Neuro-Affirming

Child-Led, Relational & Meaningful

  • Natural Reinforcers: Activities leverage the child's natural intrinsic passions rather than arbitrary edible rewards.
  • Sensory Validation: Stimming and sensory movement are recognized as vital regulation tools, never targeted for elimination.
  • Emotional Safety: Co-regulation precedes learning. A dysregulated nervous system is given sensory comfort first.
  • Total Communication: Gestures, AAC speech devices, sign, and spoken words are given equal dignity and immediate response.
Traditional Compliance Models Outdated

Repetitive, Table-Bound & Masking

  • Artificial Conditioning: Excessive table trials relying on tokens or candy that fail to generalize to real life.
  • Forced Eye Contact: Coercing non-speaking or autistic children to make eye contact despite neurological pain.
  • Sensory Ignorance: Treating sensory meltdowns as willful disobedience instead of fight-or-flight nervous system overload.
  • Speech Withholding: Denying AAC communication tablets until the child produces vocal sounds.
Clinical Instrumentation

Advanced Diagnostic Technology for Objective Measurement

We combine empathetic human care with modern clinical bio-instrumentation to measure autonomic regulation, motor coordination, and speech acoustics with scientific precision.

01

Video Gait & Motion Analysis

Multi-camera slow-motion tracking for hypotonia, toe-walking, and asymmetrical movement patterns in gross motor rehabilitation.

02

Eye-Gaze Diagnostic Tracker

Measures visual fixation, saccadic eye movements, and calibration accuracy for non-verbal high-tech communication access.

03

Acoustic Speech Spectrograms

Real-time visual waveforms showing pitch, formant frequencies, and vocal resonance to accelerate apraxia and articulation progress.

04

Heart Rate Variability (HRV)

Non-invasive autonomic monitoring inside sensory gyms to objectively identify arousal levels before sensory overload occurs.
