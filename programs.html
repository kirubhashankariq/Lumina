Our Specialized Programs

Tailored educational and therapeutic paths for holistic growth.

Learning Disabilities Support

Exclusively designed for students with Dyslexia, ADHD, and other cognitive processing challenges. We utilize elite multisensory reading programs, adaptive mathematics software, and intensive executive functioning coaching to bridge learning gaps permanently.

  • Orton-Gillingham Approach
  • 1-on-1 Academic Tutoring
  • Assistive Tech Integration

Occupational Therapy

Deeply focusing on fine motor skills, complete sensory integration, and vital daily living tasks. Our state-of-the-art sensory gym allows children to rapidly develop coordination and robust sensory processing in a completely safe, fun environment.

  • Sensory Diet Planning
  • Fine & Gross Motor Training
  • Play-Based Interventions

Life Skills Training

Empowering adolescents and young adults with the uncompromising tools for complete independence. From advanced personal hygiene to basic financial literacy and vocational prep, we prepare students for the real world.

  • Social Navigation Scenarios
  • Vocational & Pre-Vocational Prep
  • Community Integration Outings
