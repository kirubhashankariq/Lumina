Pedagogical Foundation

Universal Design for Learning (UDL) in Every Classroom

We believe the barrier is never the student—it is an inflexible curriculum. Lumina designs multi-channel learning so students can absorb, process, and demonstrate knowledge on their own terms.

01

Multiple Means of Representation

Information is presented across simultaneous visual, auditory, and tactile channels. Audio text-to-speech, graphic diagrams, concrete manipulatives, and captioned video ensure no single processing bottleneck halts comprehension.

✓ Multisensory Scaffolding
02

Multiple Means of Action & Expression

Students are never forced into single-modality testing. Mastery can be proven through spoken presentation, digital AAC speech output, video creation, graphic storyboards, or tactile prototype modeling.

✓ Flexible Mastery Verification
03

Multiple Means of Engagement

We honor special interests (trains, astronomy, coding, marine biology) as cognitive bridges to teach core math, science, and literacy, maximizing dopamine-driven intrinsic motivation.bridges to teach core math

✓ Hyperfocus & Passion Leveraging

Admissions & Funding

Three Clear Pathways to Fund Your Child’s Education & Therapy

Special education is a legal right under IDEA. Our full-time family admissions advocates help you navigate district funding vouchers, insurance reimbursement, and private tuition options.

Pathway 01

Public School District IEP Placement (NPS)

When a local public district cannot meet a student's intensive clinical IEP needs, the district may fund 100% of tuition and specialized transportation at Lumina as an Approved Non-Public School.

District Cost: $0 Parent Co-Pay
Pathway 02

Commercial Insurance Clinical Superbills

Our clinical billing desk generates detailed CPT-coded superbills (97110, 92507, 97530, 97153) for Speech, Occupational Therapy, and Behavioral Health for out-of-network reimbursement.

Reimbursement: 60% – 80% Typical
Pathway 03

Direct Enrollment & Flexible Tuition

Private pay families can choose 10-month academic tuition with flexible monthly payment plans, needs-based financial scholarship grants, and 529 education savings plan utilization.

Scholarship Assistance Available
